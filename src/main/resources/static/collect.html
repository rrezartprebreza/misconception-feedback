Information and consent

This is a research project by Rrezart Prebreza, PhD (first author), and Besart Prebreza, PhD (corresponding author), Department of Computer Science, Universum International College. It looks at how well AI models recognise the mistaken ideas behind students' answers in data structures and algorithms. [Ethics approval reference.]

  • You send one or more answers to programming exercises. You will not see feedback on this page.
  • Your answer is stored without your name, under the ID you were given. Emails and author lines in the code are removed automatically. Do not write your name or other personal data in your code or explanation.
  • Instructors read the answers and label them. Anonymised answers are then sent to an AI service (Groq) to test its detection, and the results may be published. No answer is published with your ID.
  • Taking part is voluntary and does not affect your grade. You can withdraw an answer by giving your ID to Rrezart Prebreza until [date]. Data is kept for [how long] on [where].
  • Questions: rrezart.prebreza@universum-ks.org.
